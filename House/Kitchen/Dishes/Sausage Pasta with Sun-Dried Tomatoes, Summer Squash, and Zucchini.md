### **Sausage Pasta with Sun-Dried Tomatoes, Summer Squash, and Zucchini**

A flavorful and vibrant pasta dish featuring juicy sausage, tangy sun-dried tomatoes, and tender summer squash and zucchini in a light, garlicky sauce.

#### **Ingredients** (Serves 2)

- 6 oz pasta (penne, rigatoni, or orecchiette work well)
- ½ lb Italian sausage (mild or spicy), casings removed
- 1 tbsp olive oil (or reserved oil from sun-dried tomatoes)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- ½ cup sun-dried tomatoes, thinly sliced
- 1 small summer squash, halved and sliced
- 1 small zucchini, halved and sliced
- ½ cup chicken broth (or pasta water)
- ¼ cup heavy cream (or whole milk for a lighter version)
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese, plus more for garnish
- 1 tbsp fresh basil, chopped (or 1 tsp dried basil)
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Zest of ½ lemon (optional, for brightness)

#### **Instructions**

1. **Cook the Pasta**:
    
    - Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve ½ cup of pasta water before draining.
2. **Brown the Sausage**:
    
    - Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
    - Add the sausage and break it into crumbles with a spatula. Cook until browned and cooked through, about 5-7 minutes. Transfer to a plate and set aside.
3. **Sauté the Aromatics & Veggies**:
    
    - In the same skillet, add the minced garlic and red pepper flakes, sautéing for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
    - Stir in the sun-dried tomatoes, zucchini, and summer squash. Cook for 3-4 minutes until just tender.
4. **Create the Sauce**:
    
    - Pour in the chicken broth and let it reduce slightly, about 2 minutes.
    - Lower the heat and stir in the heavy cream and Parmesan cheese, letting the sauce come together.
5. **Combine Everything**:
    
    - Return the cooked sausage to the skillet and toss to combine.
    - Add the drained pasta, tossing everything together. If needed, add a splash of reserved pasta water to loosen the sauce.
    - Season with salt, black pepper, and lemon zest (if using).
6. **Finish & Serve**:
    
    - Sprinkle fresh basil and extra Parmesan cheese over the top.
    - Serve immediately and enjoy!
