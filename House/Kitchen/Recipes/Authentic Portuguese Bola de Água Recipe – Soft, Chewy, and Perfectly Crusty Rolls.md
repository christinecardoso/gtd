Bola de Água (Portuguese Water Bread Rolls) Recipe

Bola de Água is a soft, slightly chewy Portuguese roll with a moist interior. The dough has a high hydration level, giving it a unique texture.

  

⏳ Total Time: ~3.5 hours (including rising time)

👩‍🍳 Active Time: 20–25 minutes

🥖 Yield: 6 rolls

  

Ingredients (Makes about 6 rolls)

• 500g (4 cups) bread flour

• 375ml (1 ½ cups) warm water (about 40°C/105°F)

• 7g (2 ¼ tsp) active dry yeast or 15g fresh yeast

• 10g (2 tsp) salt

• 5g (1 tsp) sugar

• 1 tbsp olive oil (optional, for extra softness)

  

  

  

Instructions

  

1. Activate the Yeast (10 minutes)

• In a small bowl, combine 375ml warm water and 5g sugar.

• Stir in 7g active dry yeast (or 15g fresh yeast) and let sit for 5–10 minutes until foamy.

  

2. Mix the Dough (10 minutes)

• In a large mixing bowl, combine 500g bread flour and 10g salt.

• Gradually add the yeast mixture while mixing with a wooden spoon or in a stand mixer with a dough hook.

• Knead for 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic. The dough will be sticky—avoid adding extra flour.

  

3. First Rise (1.5–2 hours)

• Lightly grease a bowl with olive oil (optional) and place the dough inside.

• Cover with a damp towel and let it rise for 1.5–2 hours, or until doubled in size.

  

4. Shape the Rolls (5 minutes)

• Lightly flour a work surface and divide the dough into 6 equal pieces (~140g each).

• Shape each piece into a ball by folding the edges underneath to create a smooth top.

• Place them on a parchment-lined baking sheet, leaving space between them.

  

5. Second Rise (45–60 minutes)

• Cover with a towel and let the rolls rise for 45 minutes to 1 hour until puffy.

  

6. Bake (15–18 minutes)

• Preheat oven to 230°C (450°F).

• Place a small oven-safe dish with hot water on the bottom rack to create steam.

• Bake the rolls for 15–18 minutes, until golden brown.

  

7. Cool & Serve

• Let the rolls cool on a wire rack before slicing.
