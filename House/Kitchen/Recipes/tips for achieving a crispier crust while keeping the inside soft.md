To achieve a crispier crust while keeping the inside soft, try these techniques:

  
1. Increase Steam in the Oven

• Method 1: Place a small oven-safe dish with boiling water on the bottom rack. This creates steam, delaying crust formation so the rolls expand fully.

• Method 2: Spray the rolls lightly with water just before placing them in the oven.

• Method 3: Add ice cubes to a preheated baking tray at the bottom of the oven right before baking.

  

2. Bake on a Preheated Surface

• Use a pizza stone or preheated baking sheet to help the rolls bake more evenly and develop a better crust.

  

3. Extend Baking Time Slightly

• Bake at 230°C (450°F) for the first 10 minutes, then reduce to 200°C (400°F) for the remaining time.

• Bake an extra 2–3 minutes if needed for a crispier crust, but watch closely to avoid overbrowning.

  

4. Let Them Cool on a Wire Rack

• Avoid leaving the rolls on a baking sheet after removing them from the oven. This prevents trapped steam from softening the crust.
