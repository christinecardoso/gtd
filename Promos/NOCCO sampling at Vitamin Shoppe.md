## Nocco Sampling at Vitamin Shoppe – Key Insights

### Customer Preferences

Cherry Berry emerged as the clear favorite during the Nocco sampling. While many customers initially expected to prefer Strawberry, their opinions shifted after tasting both flavors. Although they appreciated Strawberry’s taste, several noted that it had an unexpectedly sweet, almost punchy finish that didn’t resonate as well. In contrast, Cherry Berry delivered a more balanced and enjoyable flavor, making it the standout choice of the day.

### Caffeine Content & Labeling Concerns

Customers had several questions regarding caffeine content and sourcing:

• They wanted to know the exact caffeine amount, but it wasn’t clearly stated on the back of the label.

• The label mentioned caffeine but did not specify the source, leading customers to ask whether it came from a natural source.

• Since many shoppers specifically look for this information when choosing an energy drink, clearer labeling could enhance transparency and ease of purchase.

The cooler display was limited to just two rows of the product, making only two flavors visible to customers. Given the shopping habits at this location, where many customers come in specifically to grab a single energy drink, the limited selection may impact product visibility and trial opportunities.
