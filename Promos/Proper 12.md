## 3/16 St Patrick’s Crawl
At ER Bradley’s, we sampled both Proper 12 Apple shots and Proper 12 Green Tea shots, and the response was fantastic. Guests loved the swag and were enthusiastic about trying both varieties. Many asked where they could purchase a bottle and inquired about the price point, with some even mentioning they planned to stop by Total Wine afterward to pick one up.

The crowd was diverse, including Irish guests, recently retired consumers, and others celebrating various events. Everyone was in great spirits, wishing each other a happy St. Patrick’s Day. Serving the whiskey in mixed shots made it more approachable, especially for women and those who weren’t interested in taking straight shots.

Overall, the experience was incredibly positive, with high engagement and strong consumer interest in the product.
