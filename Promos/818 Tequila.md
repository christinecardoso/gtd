Here are some solid talking points for **818 Tequila Blanco** and **818 Tequila Reposado**:

### **818 Tequila Blanco**

- **Smooth & Crisp:** A bright, clean tequila with a silky mouthfeel.

- **Flavor Profile:** Notes of caramel, toasted nuts, vanilla, and light spice with a touch of oak.
- **Aroma:** Buttery smooth with hints of honey, dried fruit, and warm baking spices.
- **Smooth Finish:** A great balance between agave freshness and subtle barrel influence.
- **Perfect For:** Sipping neat or in elevated tequila-based cocktails like an Old Fashioned or a reposado margarita.

818 Tequila is made using **traditional and modern cooking methods**:

### **Cooking Process**

- The agave is **slow-cooked in brick ovens** (**hornos**) for up to **40 hours**.
- This method allows for a **gentle caramelization of the agave sugars**, which contributes to its smooth and slightly sweet profile.

### **Why Brick Ovens?**

- Traditional method used by premium tequila brands.
- Helps develop richer agave flavors compared to modern autoclaves (which use high-pressure steam for faster cooking).
- Results in a smoother and more complex taste.
