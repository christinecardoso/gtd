## On Premise, West Palm Beach St Patrick’s Crawl 3/16 
### Mr B’s
When we arrived at Mr. B’s, management was unaware of the promotion and seemed caught off guard. I showed them our booking details on PINATA, and Alaina, the bar manager, explained that no one had coordinated with her. While they did have the product on hand, they were frustrated that no setup had been arranged in advance.

Alexandra, the general manager, and Alaina expressed uncertainty about whether their current clientele would be interested in the product. They even went around asking guests for their thoughts. After some discussion, they ultimately decided to allow us to sample at a few tables and with guests seated at the bar.

At Mr. B’s, we primarily sampled the Honey Tullamore Dew, and consumer engagement was positive.

### ER Bradley’s
At ER Bradley’s, the manager instructed the bartender to make Green Tea shots using Tullamore Dew. We served them in regular shot glasses, but the bartender also brought out fun test tube sample cups for an initial round of tastings.

The response was overwhelmingly positive—people were excited to try them. The Green Tea shots made the whiskey more approachable for all guests, including those who might not have otherwise considered sampling, particularly women. This approach helped engage a broader audience and created a more inviting tasting experience.
