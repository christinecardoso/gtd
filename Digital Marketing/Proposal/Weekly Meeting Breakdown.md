## **Weekly Meeting Breakdown:**

1. **Week 1 - Content Strategy & Planning (60 minutes)**
    
    - **Purpose**: Discuss overall strategy for the upcoming month, brainstorm new content ideas, review target audience, set goals for the content, and ensure alignment with broader marketing goals.
        
    - **Why 60 minutes?**: This meeting involves setting the tone for the month, aligning priorities, and creating a roadmap for content creation. It may require a deeper discussion of objectives and resources.
        
2. **Week 2 - Content Review & Adjustment (30 minutes)**
    
    - **Purpose**: Review the progress of content created so far (blog posts, social media), and make any necessary adjustments based on feedback or performance insights.
        
    - **Why 30 minutes?**: A quick check-in to ensure everything is progressing as expected and to make minor adjustments to the content plan.
        
3. **Week 3 - Social Media Focus & Engagement Strategy (60 minutes)**
    
    - **Purpose**: Dive into social media performance and strategy, review analytics, and plan out upcoming posts or campaigns. Discuss any new trends, tools, or strategies to improve engagement.
        
    - **Why 60 minutes?**: Social media is dynamic, so this meeting needs time to analyze engagement, plan next steps, and adjust strategies based on metrics.
        
4. **Week 4 - Final Content Review & Preparation for Next Month (30 minutes)**
    
    - **Purpose**: Go over any final content pieces for the month (blog posts, newsletters, etc.), review the success of content in the past month, and prepare for the next month’s content.
        
    - **Why 30 minutes?**: This meeting should focus on wrapping up the current month's work and quickly aligning on content for the upcoming month.
        

---
