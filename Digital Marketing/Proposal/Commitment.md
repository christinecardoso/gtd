For the structure of your agreement, you have two main options: a **commitment** (fixed-term contract) or a **month-to-month** (flexible contract). The choice between the two depends on the client's preferences and your business needs.

### **1. Month-to-Month Contract:**

- **Flexibility for the Client**: This option allows both parties to exit the contract at any time without long-term commitments. The client can cancel services after a given notice period (e.g., 30 days), and you can also adjust your workload and pricing as needed.
    
- **Pros**: More flexibility for the client and the service provider. It can be attractive to clients who are unsure of the long-term needs or those with uncertain budget cycles.
    
- **Cons**: It can be less stable for you as the service provider. Clients may cancel with short notice, creating gaps in your schedule.
    

**Example of Month-to-Month Terms** (to include in the contract):

- This agreement will be on a **month-to-month** basis, with services provided for an agreed fee of **$2,000/month** (or equivalent in GBP).
    
- Either party may terminate the agreement by providing **30 days' written notice** before the end of the current month.
    
- The client will pay for the month in which services are rendered, with no long-term commitment required.
    

---

### **2. Commitment (Fixed-Term Contract):**

- **Long-Term Stability**: This option locks the client into a contract for a set period (e.g., 6 months or 1 year). It ensures steady work for you as the service provider and may offer better rates or additional services as an incentive.
    
- **Pros**: Long-term stability, predictable cash flow, and the ability to plan for ongoing work.
    
- **Cons**: The client may feel less flexibility, and it might be harder to attract clients who are hesitant to commit to a long-term contract.
    

**Example of Commitment Terms** (to include in the contract):

- This agreement is a **6-month commitment**. The client agrees to pay **$2,000/month** (or equivalent in GBP) for the duration of the contract.
    
- After the initial term, the contract will automatically renew on a **month-to-month** basis unless either party provides **30 days' written notice** to terminate.
    
- If the client wishes to terminate the agreement before the end of the fixed term, an **early termination fee** of **1 month’s service fee** will apply.
    

---

### **3. Hybrid Option (Combination of Both)**:

- You could also offer a **hybrid model**: a **minimum commitment period** (e.g., 3 months or 6 months) followed by **month-to-month renewal**.
    
- This allows you to lock in a certain period of work while still maintaining flexibility for both parties after the initial commitment.
    

**Example of Hybrid Terms** (to include in the contract):

- This agreement has a **minimum 3-month commitment** with a monthly fee of **$2,000/month** (or equivalent in GBP).
    
- After the initial 3 months, the agreement will transition to a **month-to-month** arrangement unless terminated by either party with **30 days' written notice**.
    

---

### **In the Email:**

You can outline the **month-to-month** option or mention that you're open to discussing a fixed-term agreement if the client prefers it.

## For example:

## **Email Example (Month-to-Month Option)**:

**Commitment**:  
This agreement would be on a **month-to-month basis**, with services provided at **$2,000/month** (or equivalent in GBP). Either party can terminate the agreement with **30 days’ written notice**, providing flexibility without any long-term commitment.

If you'd prefer a fixed-term contract, I’d be happy to discuss terms that align with your needs.

---

## **Email Example (Fixed-Term or Hybrid Option)**:

**Commitment**:  
The agreement would be for a **minimum 3-month commitment**, with services provided at **$2,000/month** (or equivalent in GBP). After the initial 3-month period, it would transition to **month-to-month** with the option to continue or cancel with **30 days’ written notice**.

---

By including the commitment structure in your email and contract, you clarify the terms of the partnership and manage expectations, whether the client prefers flexibility or stability.
