For clarity and professionalism, it's important to include **payment terms** in both the **email** and the **proposal/contract**. The payment terms should outline how and when payments will be collected, preferred payment methods, invoicing details, and any other necessary payment-related information.

### **Payment Terms to Include:**

#### **1. Payment Method**:

- **Accepted Payments**: Specify the payment methods you accept. In this case, Stripe is a great choice, but you could also mention PayPal or direct bank transfer if applicable.
    
- **Currency**: Since the business is in the UK, mention that payments will be accepted in GBP (£) unless otherwise stated.
    

#### **2. Payment Schedule**:

- **Monthly Retainer**: Clarify that the client will pay **$2,000/month** (or the equivalent in GBP, if necessary).
    
- **Invoice Date**: Specify the date of invoicing (e.g., at the start of each month or after content has been delivered).
    
- **Due Date**: State when payments are due (e.g., within 7 days of the invoice date).
    
- **Late Payments**: Outline any penalties for late payments, such as interest charges or a late fee.
    

#### **3. Invoice Details**:

- **Invoice Frequency**: Monthly, based on the agreed-upon services.
    
- **Invoice Information**: Include that you will provide a **detailed invoice** that breaks down all services rendered during the month (e.g., 2 blog posts, social media posts, etc.).
    
- **Payment Instructions**: Provide the details for making payments through Stripe (or other methods), such as a payment link or instructions for direct bank transfer.
    

#### **4. Refund Policy**:

- Clarify whether you offer any refunds, particularly if work is completed but the client is unsatisfied. You can mention that the work will be reviewed by both parties before invoicing.
    

#### **5. Taxes**:

- If applicable, mention whether **VAT** or any other taxes are included in the payment or need to be added.
    

---

### **Example Payment Terms (to include in email and proposal)**:

---

#### **Email (short version)**:

---

**Payment Terms**:  
For payments, I accept **Stripe**, which will allow for fast and secure processing. Invoices will be issued **monthly** at the beginning of each month, with payment due within **7 days** of receiving the invoice.

Should you prefer an alternative payment method, please let me know, and we can accommodate accordingly.

---

#### **Proposal/Contract (detailed version)**:

---

**Payment Terms:**

1. **Payment Method**:
    
    - Payments will be processed via **Stripe** (or another method if specified) for seamless transactions.
        
    - If preferred, **PayPal** or **direct bank transfers** can be arranged.
        
    - All payments will be made in **GBP (£)**.
        
2. **Invoicing & Schedule**:
    
    - The client will be invoiced monthly for the amount of **$2,000 (or equivalent in GBP)**.
        
    - **Invoices** will be issued **on the 1st of each month** or upon delivery of work, whichever comes first.
        
    - **Payment is due** within **7 days** of receiving the invoice.
        
3. **Late Payments**:
    
    - If payment is not received within **7 days** of the invoice date, a **late fee** of **5% per week** will be applied to the outstanding balance.
        
4. **Refunds**:
    
    - As services are rendered on a monthly basis, no refunds will be issued for completed work. Any concerns or dissatisfaction should be communicated within 5 days of the invoice to allow for any necessary adjustments or revisions before payment.
        
5. **Taxes**:
    
    - All fees are **exclusive of VAT** unless otherwise stated. Any VAT or applicable taxes will be added to the total amount due.
        

---

By including **payment terms** in both the email and the contract, you provide clear guidelines on how you expect payments to be handled. It sets expectations for the client and ensures you have a structured process in place for receiving compensation.
