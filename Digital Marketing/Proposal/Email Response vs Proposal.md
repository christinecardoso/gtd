For clarity and professionalism, it’s best to keep the **email** focused on the immediate, high-level details and encourage the recipient to review the **full proposal or contract** for all the detailed terms and scope. The **email** should include the essential elements that can be quickly understood, while the **proposal or contract** will house the more formal and detailed breakdown of the scope, terms, and conditions.

### **What to Include in the Email:**

1. **Introduction & Acknowledgment**:
    - Thank the recipient for their inquiry and confirm understanding of their needs or budget.
        
2. **High-Level Offer**:
    - Mention the $2,000/month package and the **main deliverables** (e.g., 2 blog posts, social media content, newsletter).
    - Highlight the **main inclusions** (like content creation, weekly meetings, basic revisions, etc.).
        
3. **Key Points on Publishing/Scheduling (if applicable)**:
    - Briefly mention whether publishing and scheduling are included in the $2,000/month price or if it will be handled by the client.
        
4. **Timeline for Response**:
    - Mention how quickly you respond to emails/messages (e.g., within 24-48 hours).
        
5. **CTA (Call to Action)**:
    - Invite the recipient to review the full proposal or contract for all details.
    - Suggest a call or meeting to finalize the details and answer any questions.
        

---

### **What to Include in the Proposal or Contract:**

1. **Detailed Breakdown of Scope**:
    
    - Full list of deliverables (e.g., blog posts, social media posts, newsletters, etc.).
    - Clear description of what’s **included** and what’s **not included** (e.g., publishing, paid promotion, performance reporting).
    - Breakdown of hours dedicated to each task (e.g., 8-12 social media posts = X hours, newsletters = X hours).
        
2. **Payment Terms**:
    - Total fee of $2,000/month and any **additional fees** for extra services (e.g., performance monitoring, paid promotions).
    - **Hourly rate** for extra work outside the scope (e.g., £100/hour).
        
3. **Approval and Revision Process**:
    - Details about the revision policy (e.g., 2 rounds of revisions).
    - Timeline for feedback and approval processes for content.
        
4. **Communication**:
    - Define the expected **response time** (e.g., 24-48 hours).
    - Mention weekly meeting schedules and any additional meetings.
        
5. **Content Calendar**:
    - Explain the process for creating and **managing** the content calendar.
        
6. **Timeline for Deliverables**:
    - Define the **timeline for each type of content** and when they will be published or delivered.
        
7. **Performance Tracking & Reporting (if applicable)**:
    - If not included, clearly state what type of performance monitoring/reporting can be offered and its cost.
        
8. **Contractual Terms**:
    - A formal agreement on **scope**, **budget**, **terms of service**, **cancellation policy**, and any **legal clauses**.

For **$2,000 per month**, I would provide:

- **Two high-quality blog posts** per month (research, writing, SEO optimization).

Please note that publishing and scheduling content can either be **included** for an additional fee, or you can manage it on your end, depending on your preference. We can also explore **performance tracking** or **paid promotion** as additional services if needed.

For more detailed terms (including payment, approvals, and scope breakdown), I’ve attached a proposal that outlines everything in full.

Let me know if you’d like to schedule a call to discuss further. I’m happy to adjust this proposal to better fit your needs!

Best,  
Christine
