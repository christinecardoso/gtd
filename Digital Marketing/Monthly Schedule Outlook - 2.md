**Monthly Schedule Outlook** (with Weekly Social Media Content Production):

1. **Week 1: Content Strategy & Planning**
    
    - **Kick-off Meeting (if needed)**: A quick call to discuss priorities, content themes, and key goals for the upcoming month.
        
    - **Content Calendar**: I’ll send over a content calendar outlining blog topics, social media post themes, and the newsletter, with deadlines and publishing schedules for the entire month.
        
    - **Approval on Content Plan**: You’ll review and approve the topics and schedule for the month.
        
2. **Week 2: Content Creation (Blog Posts, Newsletter, and Social Media Posts)**
    
    - **Blog & Newsletter**: I will create the first blog post and newsletter draft early in the week for your review. You’ll have a few days (3-4 days) for feedback and approval.
        
    - **Social Media Posts**: I will start working on the first batch of social media posts for the week, focusing on content creation, copywriting, and visual elements. Each week, I will draft **3-4 posts** for approval.
        
        - For example, Monday: Draft social media posts for the week.
            
        - Tuesday: Send social media drafts for feedback.
            
        - Wednesday/Thursday: Implement feedback and finalize posts.
            
3. **Week 3: Content Finalization & Adjustments**
    
    - **Revisions for Blog & Newsletter**: Based on your feedback from Week 2, I will make final revisions to the blog post and newsletter.
        
    - **Social Media Approval**: Once social media posts are approved, I’ll schedule them for posting. This includes final adjustments and preparing visuals if needed. Posts for the week are published on your preferred platforms.
        
    - **Review & Feedback**: If any final adjustments are needed for the blog or newsletter, I’ll handle them promptly.
        
4. **Week 4: Content Finalization, Reporting & Publishing**
    
    - **Social Media Posts**: Continue to finalize and publish weekly social media posts, ensuring content consistency across platforms.
        
    - **Content Reporting**: If performance tracking is required, I can provide basic analytics for the month’s content (this can be discussed separately if you'd like more in-depth reporting).
        
    - **Newsletter Distribution**: Ensure the newsletter is scheduled for distribution or sent out.
        

---

**Weekly Cycle for Social Media**:

- **Monday**: Draft the 3-4 posts for the week and send them over for feedback.
    
- **Tuesday**: Revise posts based on feedback.
    
- **Wednesday/Thursday**: Final approval, and schedule posts for the week.
    
- **Friday**: Monitor posts and prepare for the next week’s content.
