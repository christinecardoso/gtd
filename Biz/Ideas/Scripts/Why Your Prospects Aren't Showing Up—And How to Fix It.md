To integrate the **VSSL call prep** and other **sales call optimization tasks** into your **project management system and calendar**, follow these steps:

---

## **Step 1: Create a Task List for Each Sales Call**

For every booked sales call, break it down into **repeatable, structured tasks** in your **project management tool** (e.g., ClickUp, Trello, Asana, Notion, or Monday.com).

### **Task Template for Sales Call Prep:**

1️⃣ **Send Personalized Voice Note/Video** (1 day before)

- Task: "Record & send personalized voice note/video for [Client Name]"
    
- Due Date: **1 day before the call**
    
- Assigned To: **You or your sales team**
    

2️⃣ **Increase Perceived Value (Pre-Call Bonus Setup)** (1-2 days before)

- Task: "Prepare pre-call value bonus (e.g., Google review audit, database reactivation, social proof examples)"
    
- Due Date: **1-2 days before the call**
    
- Subtasks:
    
    - Identify relevant bonus for the client
        
    - Prepare messaging for the bonus offer
        

3️⃣ **Create & Send VSSL Pre-Call Video** (2 days before)

- Task: "Record & send VSSL video for [Client Name]"
    
- Due Date: **2 days before the call**
    
- Subtasks:
    
    - Record video covering expectations, case studies, and urgency
        
    - Upload to a platform (YouTube unlisted, Loom, Google Drive, etc.)
        
    - Send link via email or SMS
        

4️⃣ **Reminder Call (1-2 hours before the meeting)**

- Task: "Call [Client Name] to confirm sales call & build rapport"
    
- Due Date: **1-2 hours before the meeting**
    
- Notes: Add a quick **call script** for reminders
    

---

## **Step 2: Automate Tasks in Your Calendar & CRM**

Use **automations** in tools like **Zapier, HubSpot, Pipedrive, or Calendly** to **auto-create** these tasks when a sales call is booked.

### **Automation Examples:**

✅ **When a new sales call is scheduled → Auto-create a task list in your project management system**  
✅ **Send VSSL video reminder email 24 hours before the call**  
✅ **Send SMS reminder 1 hour before the call**

---

## **Step 3: Integrate With Your Calendar**

- **Google Calendar/Outlook:**
    
    - Add a **task list link** to the calendar event so everything is in one place.
        
    - Set reminders for **VSSL video sending, value prep, and follow-ups.**
        
- **Calendly & HubSpot Integration:**
    
    - Auto-send VSSL video link & social proof in confirmation emails.
