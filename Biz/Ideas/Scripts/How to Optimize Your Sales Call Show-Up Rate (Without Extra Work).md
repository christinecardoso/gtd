## **2. Automate & Reduce Repetitive Tasks**

Instead of manually preparing for every call, create an **automated sales call prep workflow**.

### **✅ Step 1: Use a Task Template for Every Call**

Most project management tools let you **save task templates**. Instead of creating tasks from scratch, **auto-generate tasks for every new call**.

📌 **Example: In ClickUp or Asana → Set Up a "Sales Call Prep" Template**

- **Pre-recorded VSSL video link** (use the same video for most prospects)
    
- **Automated email with social proof & bonus offer**
    
- **Call reminder task auto-created 1 hour before the meeting**
    

---

### **✅ Step 2: Automate Pre-Call Emails & Reminders**

Use **Zapier** to trigger emails & reminders **without manual effort**.

**Example Automation:**

- 🗓️ **New call booked in Calendly → Zapier triggers a sequence:**
    
    1. Auto-create tasks in **ClickUp/Asana**
        
    2. Send a **personalized pre-call email** with VSSL video
        
    3. Schedule a **reminder SMS** via **Twilio**
        
    4. Add the prospect to **HubSpot or Pipedrive CRM**
        

**Bonus:** Add a **pre-call survey** using **Typeform or Google Forms** to qualify prospects before the call.

---

### **✅ Step 3: Batch Record & Reuse Content**

You don’t need to **record a new VSSL video** for every call. Instead, create a **generalized pre-call video** covering:

- **What to expect on the call**
    
- **Common objections & how they’re solved**
    
- **Case studies & success stories**
    
- **The offer & next steps**
    

🎥 **Host it on Loom or YouTube (unlisted) & send the same link** to every prospect.

🔹 **Pro Tip:** If you want some personalization, record a quick **15-second intro clip** and attach it to the **standard video.**

---

### **✅ Step 4: Automate Post-Call Follow-Ups**

After the call, follow-up should also be **automated** to nurture leads.

**Example Follow-Up Automation:**

- **Immediately After Call** → Send recap email via HubSpot/Pipedrive
    
- **24 Hours Later** → Send additional value (case study, freebie, etc.)
    
- **3 Days Later** → Send a soft follow-up: “Do you have any questions?”
    
- **7 Days Later** → Final follow-up with urgency (limited spots, offer ending, etc.)
    

🔹 **Pro Tip:** If they **don’t** show up to the call, **automatically trigger a reschedule email**.

---

## **3. How to Scale & Never Repeat This Manually**

✅ **Batch Content Creation:**

- Record VSSL videos **once per quarter** instead of every time
    
- Pre-make multiple **bonus offers** (Google reviews, reactivations, etc.)
    

✅ **Automate Client Onboarding & Pre-Call Sequences**

- Use **Zapier + HubSpot/Calendly** to auto-send emails, videos, and bonuses
    
- Set up a **pre-call nurture sequence** (emails & texts)
    

✅ **Delegate Tasks Using a VA or AI Assistant**

- Have a VA or AI-powered tool **personalize voice notes**
    
- Use AI-based tools like **ChatGPT or Jasper** for email follow-ups
