### **Day 1: Messaging – Get Your Ideal Customer to Pay Attention**

**[Cut to title slide: "Day 1: Messaging"]**

**Host (voiceover with b-roll of ad examples):**  
"Messaging is everything. You could have the best product in the world, but if your message doesn’t resonate with your audience, it’s not going to matter. So, how do you create messages that grab attention and make people stop scrolling?"

**[Cut to host speaking directly to camera]**

**Host:**  
"First, **clarify who you’re talking to**. Are you speaking to a busy mom? A fitness enthusiast? A professional looking to level up? Understanding their **pain points** and **desires** is key."

**[Show on screen: Examples of good ad headlines with visuals]**  
"Your messaging should clearly communicate **how you solve a problem** or **address a desire**. For example, instead of saying ‘Buy our product’, say ‘Tired of dealing with X? We have a solution for you.’ This language speaks directly to their needs."

**[Cut to host again]**  
"Also, make sure you’re using **simple, clear language**. Remember, **clarity over creativity**. The headline is crucial—make it bold, intriguing, and to the point."

**[On-screen: Examples of good vs. bad messaging]**  
"Finally, every message should have a **strong Call to Action (CTA)**. Don’t leave them wondering what to do next. Use phrases like, ‘Get your free consultation today!’ or ‘Shop now to get 20% off!’"

### **Day 4: Testing – Let Data Guide Your Decisions**

**[Cut to title slide: "Day 4: Testing"]**

**Host (voiceover with on-screen ad performance data):**  
"On Day 4, we’re diving into **testing**. You have to use **data** to make decisions about your ads. Without testing, you’re flying blind."

**[Cut to host speaking directly to camera]**

**Host:**  
"Start by doing **A/B testing**. Test one element at a time—whether it’s the image, headline, CTA, or targeting—and see what works best."

**[On-screen: Example of A/B test results]**  
"Once you’ve tested, look at metrics like **CTR** (Click-Through Rate), **Conversion Rate**, and **CPA** (Cost Per Acquisition). These will tell you which ads are performing well and which need tweaking."

**[Cut to host holding a tablet showing test results]**  
"Make sure you track **key metrics** to understand which creatives are bringing in the best ROI, and adjust your approach accordingly."
