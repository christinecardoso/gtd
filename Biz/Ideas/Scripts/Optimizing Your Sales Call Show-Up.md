# **Optimizing Your Sales Call Show-Up Rate**

Improving your show-up rate is critical for maximizing conversions and ensuring your sales calls are as effective as possible. Below are four proven strategies to increase attendance and engagement while positioning yourself as a trusted authority.

[[Sales Call Topics to explore]]
---

## **1. Send Personalized Voice Notes or Videos Before the Call**

A simple yet powerful way to build rapport before the call is to send a personalized voice note or video message on the day of the scheduled meeting.

### **Why This Works:**

- **Establishes professionalism and goodwill** from the very beginning.
    
- Creates a **personal connection**, making the prospect feel valued.
    
- Differentiates you from competitors who rely on generic email reminders.
    
- Reduces skepticism and increases trust before they even hop on the call.
    

### **What to Include:**

- A warm, friendly introduction.
    
- A quick reminder of the scheduled time and what they can expect.
    
- A statement reinforcing the value of the call (e.g., “I’m excited to show you how we’ve helped [industry] professionals like yourself get more clients effortlessly.”).
    

### **Common Mistakes to Avoid:**

- **Sounding robotic or generic.** This should feel like a message just for them.
    
- **Poor-quality audio or video.** Ensure clarity and professionalism.
    
- **Rushing through the message.** Speak with confidence and warmth.
    

---

## **2. Increase the Perceived Value of the Sales Call**

If prospects don’t see immediate value in showing up, they won’t. Your goal is to make the call seem like a **must-attend event** rather than just another meeting.

### **Key Factors Prospects Consider Before Showing Up:**

- **Can this person actually help me get more clients and grow my business?**
    
- **Is this someone I trust and enjoy speaking with?**
    
- **Is this worth my time, or is it just another sales pitch?**
    

### **How to Increase Perceived Value:**

#### **A. Use Social Proof**

- Share success stories of past clients in their industry.
    
- Mention testimonials or case studies to showcase real-world results.
    

#### **B. Offer Free Value Before the Call**

Providing value upfront creates a sense of **reciprocity and goodwill.** Some ideas:

- **A complimentary Google review campaign** (if applicable).
    
- **A database reactivation service** (free of charge).
    
- **An exclusive industry insight or trend report** they wouldn’t get elsewhere.
    

By offering free value, you shift the focus away from selling and toward **helping**, making the call feel like a valuable opportunity rather than a high-pressure pitch.

---

## **3. Send a 10-20 Minute Pre-Call Video**

A well-crafted pre-call video can **prime the prospect** by reinforcing your authority and the importance of the meeting.

### **What This Video Should Accomplish:**

✅ Position you as the **go-to expert** in your field.  
✅ Instill **limiting beliefs**—showing them why they **can’t** do this on their own.  
✅ Build anticipation for the call by outlining **what they will gain**.  
✅ Tap into emotional drivers, making them feel **urgency and fear of missing out (FOMO).**

### **Essential Video Structure:**

1. **Your Background & Expertise** – Why should they trust you?
    
2. **Social Proof & Success Stories** – Demonstrate results with real examples.
    
3. **What They’ll Get from the Call** – Clearly outline the benefits.
    
4. **Psychological Framing:**
    
    - _Dream Outcome Storytelling:_ Share a case study of a client who struggled but transformed their business using your system.
        
    - _Fear-Based Storytelling:_ Highlight the **consequences of inaction** (e.g., staying stuck at one listing per month and limiting their income).
        
5. **Clear Call to Action:** “Make sure to show up so you don’t miss out on this opportunity.”
    

By watching this video, the prospect will enter the call **already convinced** of your value, making it easier to close the deal.

---

## **4. Call the Prospect Beforehand**

A simple pre-call touchpoint can **dramatically** improve your show-up rate.

### **Why This Works:**

- Reinforces **that you care about their success.**
    
- Strengthens your **personal connection**, making them less likely to ghost you.
    
- Allows you to **handle objections** before the call even starts.
    

### **How to Structure This Call:**

✅ **Start with something personal** – Reference a detail from your previous conversation.  
✅ **Express excitement** – “I’m really looking forward to our call because I have something valuable to share that I think you’ll love.”  
✅ **Reinforce urgency and scarcity** – “This strategy is helping people just like you see massive results, and I can’t wait to break it down for you.”  
✅ **Confirm the meeting details** – “Just checking—are we still good for [time]?”

This **small but powerful step** reassures the prospect and increases the likelihood they will attend.

---

## **Final Thoughts: Making Every Sales Call Count**

Implementing these four strategies will not only boost your show-up rate but also set the stage for **higher conversions and stronger client relationships**.

✔ **Personalized outreach** builds trust and engagement.  
✔ **Increasing perceived value** makes your call feel like a must-attend event.  
✔ **Pre-call videos** position you as an authority and pre-sell your value.  
✔ **Pre-call check-ins** reinforce commitment and eliminate doubts.

By integrating these into your sales process, you’ll notice a **significant improvement in both attendance and close rates**—leading to more clients and greater revenue. 🚀
