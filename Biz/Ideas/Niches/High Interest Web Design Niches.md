Here are some specific web design niches that are not as oversaturated but still offer potential for profitability and high interest:

1. **Senior Living and Elder Care Websites**  
    As the population ages, the need for websites catering to senior living homes, retirement communities, and elder care services grows. This niche requires sensitivity and a focus on accessibility, which can be a selling point for specialized web designers.
    
2. **Sustainable and Eco-Friendly Businesses**  
    With increasing interest in sustainability, businesses focusing on eco-friendly products and services are on the rise. Web designers who specialize in creating websites for green, sustainable businesses have the opportunity to tap into this growing, but not yet oversaturated, market.
    
3. **Pet Services and Products**  
    Pet grooming, pet boarding, pet insurance, and pet training services need professional websites to attract customers. With pet ownership increasing worldwide, this niche is still relatively untapped in terms of specialized, high-quality web design.
    
4. **Artisans and Handmade Goods**  
    Small businesses focused on handmade goods (e.g., jewelry, custom furniture, crafts) often need custom websites that reflect their unique, high-quality offerings. This niche requires visually engaging, creative websites and can command premium pricing.
    
5. **Outdoor and Adventure Travel**  
    While tourism is a competitive market, there’s still plenty of room in niches like eco-tourism, adventure travel, hiking companies, or boutique tour operators that offer unique, personalized experiences. These businesses often need a mix of e-commerce and booking systems on their websites.
    
6. **Alternative Medicine and Wellness Practices**  
    Many wellness centers, holistic health professionals, acupuncturists, chiropractors, and naturopaths are looking for specialized websites that promote their services. There is still room in this niche to create websites with a strong focus on the customer experience, booking features, and wellness content.
    
7. **Custom Merchandise and Print-on-Demand**  
    While e-commerce is a crowded space, print-on-demand and custom merchandise (such as custom t-shirts, mugs, and posters) are still expanding. Offering web design services that focus on creative product showcases, customization features, and seamless integration with fulfillment services can be a profitable niche.
    
8. **Microbrewery and Craft Spirits Websites**  
    The craft beer and spirits industry is still growing, and many small breweries or distilleries need custom websites to showcase their unique brands and products. This niche offers potential for unique web design that reflects the personality and ethos of the brand.
    
9. **Personal Fitness and Niche Gyms**  
    Specialized fitness businesses like yoga studios, CrossFit gyms, or even niche personal trainers are looking for ways to stand out online. Offering web design focused on booking, membership management, and showcasing fitness content can attract long-term clients in this space.
    
10. **Cultural and Heritage-Based Businesses**  
    Businesses that focus on specific cultural or heritage-related products or services, such as ethnic food markets, cultural centers, or niche craft products, are becoming more prominent but are still underserved. Creating custom websites that reflect these unique identities can appeal to a growing market.
    
11. **Smart Home and IoT (Internet of Things)**  
    As the smart home and IoT industry grows, businesses that install or sell smart home products require websites that integrate with their tech and display product features in an intuitive, user-friendly manner. This niche is innovative but not overcrowded, with an increasing demand for well-designed, functional websites.
    
12. **Legal Cannabis Industry**  
    With cannabis becoming legal in more places, businesses in this industry (dispensaries, growers, equipment suppliers) are growing rapidly. Specialized web design for this market often includes e-commerce features, educational content, and an emphasis on compliance, which makes it a profitable but not overly saturated niche.
    

By focusing on these niches, web designers can carve out a specific area of expertise and offer highly specialized services, catering to businesses with unique needs that aren't as widely addressed by the general web design market.
