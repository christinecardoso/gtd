## 🎙️ EXTENSIONS & EXPERIENCES

### 1. **Audio Tours / Podcasts**

Each category (e.g., _tiled steps_) becomes a short 3–5 min episode. You can record:

- Where to find the best examples in each city
    
- Stories from residents or property owners
    
- Interviews with local architects, preservationists, or historians
    

**Format ideas:**

- “The Best Bougainvillea in Palm Beach”
    
- “The Lost Art of Coral Rock Walls”
    
- “How to Spot a Mizner Home”
    

### 2. **Interactive Maps**

Pin each photo with GPS on a digital map—visitors can:

- Filter by interest (e.g., Spanish Colonial Revival homes with wrought iron)
    
- Build custom walking or driving tours
    

### 3. **Short-Form Video Series**

Each category becomes a reel/TikTok:

- “3 Iconic Entryways You’ll Only Find in Palm Beach”
    
- “Walk With Me: Tiled Staircases of El Cid”
    
- “Curb Appeal Crush: A House with a 1930s Storybook Door”
